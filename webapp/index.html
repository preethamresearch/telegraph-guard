Testnet only (Base Sepolia). Every verdict is a live paid screening over x402 — no demo mode, no cached inference — and every signal links to an on-chain receipt. The storefronts are fictional; their destinations are real — the flagged merchant settles to an OFAC-sanctioned Tornado Cash address, the trusted one to a mainnet router — so the miners are genuinely tested rather than told what to say.
