Testnet only (Base Sepolia). Every verdict on this page is a live, paid screening — no demo mode, no cached inference — and every signal carries an on-chain hash you can open.

The two storefronts are fictional, but their payment destinations are not: the flagged merchant settles to an OFAC-sanctioned Tornado Cash address and hosts on a live URLhaus-listed malware URL; the trusted merchant settles to a well-known mainnet router. Real targets are used so the miners are genuinely tested rather than told what to say.

Accuracy is bounded by miner coverage — on a 39-target labelled corpus this scored 37 correct, missing one sanctioned address and false-positiving one router. Both were miner coverage gaps, not aggregation errors.
