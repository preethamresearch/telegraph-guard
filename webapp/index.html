Vessel shops on its own and pays from its own wallet. Before it signs anything, TelegraphGuard screens the destination across live Telegraph miners — and every verdict carries an on-chain receipt.

Type a task on the left to start. Screenings are real paid calls (~$0.01 each); the storefronts are fictional, their payment destinations are not.

Testnet only (Base Sepolia). Every verdict is a live paid screening over x402 — no demo mode, no cached inference. The flagged storefront settles to an OFAC-sanctioned Tornado Cash address; the trusted one to a well-known mainnet router — real targets, so the miners are genuinely tested rather than told what to say. On a 39-target labelled corpus this scored 37 correct; the two misses were miner coverage gaps, published in the repo rather than hidden.
